Statistical significance was assessed via paired t-tests across the 5 cross-validation folds for each pairwise comparison between the six experimental conditions. To account for multiple comparisons, Bonferroni correction was applied at family-wise α=0.05 (yielding a per-comparison threshold of α=0.0033 for the 15 pairwise tests within each metric). The Stone ROI degradation effect remained significant after Bonferroni correction (Plain ResNet50 full vs ROI: p=0.0014). Cyst ROI improvement was significant in 7 of 15 pairwise comparisons at uncorrected α=0.05 but did not survive Bonferroni correction with n=5 folds.
